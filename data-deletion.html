Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Home Privacy Policy

How to request deletion

If you are a CrowdReply user and want data associated with your use of CrowdReply deleted, open an issue in the public CrowdReply GitHub repository and state that you are requesting data deletion. Do not include access tokens, API keys, passwords or other secrets in the issue.

What can be deleted

Deletion can include locally stored comment records, draft replies, reply status information and other CrowdReply application data associated with the relevant page or account.

Revoke Meta access

You can also revoke CrowdReply's access to your Facebook Page at any time through your Meta/Facebook business integration or app settings.

Timing

Valid deletion requests will be reviewed and completed as soon as reasonably practical.
